Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This app connects a household's own Google account and Google Nest devices to that household's private, self-hosted Home Assistant system.

What data is accessed

Only the device data and controls the account owner approves on the Google consent screen (such as thermostat, camera and doorbell status and controls).

How data is used

Data is used only to display and control the owner's own devices inside their own Home Assistant system.

Storage and sharing

Data stays on the owner's own Home Assistant system. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google sign-in consent screen.
